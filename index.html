Multilingual Healthcare Access & Safety Assistant

Breaking language barriers in emergency healthcare with edge AI, real-time voice translation, and clinical triage.

The Problem Statement

Language barriers in emergency healthcare access directly endanger patient lives, delay triage, and lead to medical misdiagnoses.

Impact on Patient Safety

Patients unable to speak English cannot accurately communicate pain type, onset, or medical history, increasing mistranslation risks and treatment errors.

Target Beneficiaries

Rural & regional patients, migrant laborers, duty doctors in government hospitals, triage nurses, emergency paramedics, and medical NGOs.

Current Bottlenecks

Relying on untrained family members for translation, delayed care while searching for translators, and inability to read prescription instructions.

What was Missing?

A real-time, safety-first multilingual AI engine with medical ontology awareness, automatic ICD-10 categorization, and offline emergency guidance.

The ArogyaVani Solution

An AI-powered multilingual healthcare assistant providing seamless two-way translation, instantaneous triage categorization, and automated 108 emergency alerts.

Core End-to-End Workflow:
🗣️ Patient Native Voice/Text
➔
🧠 ArogyaVani AI NLP + Triage + RAG
➔
🩺 Doctor Portal Clinical English & ICD-10
➔
🛡️ Safe Care 108 SOS & Translated Rx

6-Layer Technical Architecture

Layer 1
Input Layer: Multilingual speech/text capture via Web Speech API and regional keyboards (Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, English).
Layer 2
Data Processing: NLP pipeline tokenization, intent recognition, entity extraction, and symptom severity scoring.
Layer 3
AI / ML Engine: Fine-tuned Multilingual LLM + Medical Ontology (SNOMED-CT / ICD-10 taxonomy).
Layer 4
Backend Services: Edge RAG inference engine, secure REST APIs, and encrypted in-memory patient triage queue.
Layer 5
Frontend Interfaces: Mobile Web App for patients & Real-time Clinical Dashboard for doctors/hospital staff.
Layer 6
Deployment: Hybrid cloud infrastructure with offline edge fallback for remote clinic operation.
Fine-tuned Multilingual LLM + Medical RAG

Grounds all clinical translations in verified medical ontologies so vernacular slang (e.g. "நெஞ்சு அடைப்பு") translates into standard clinical terms ("Acute Angina / Myocardial Infarction").

Context-Aware Anomaly Detection

Continuously analyzes SpO2 drops, abnormal BP, and high pyrexia to elevate patient triage code automatically without human delay.

WHO Guideline Compliance

All automated advice strictly adheres to World Health Organization (WHO) and ICMR first-aid protocols, prohibiting dangerous self-medication recommendations.

Novelty: Safety-First Healthcare Assistant

Unlike generic translation tools (Google Translate) which often mistranslate critical medical terms, ArogyaVani validates contraindications and dispatches emergency SOS automatically.
